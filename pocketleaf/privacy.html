PocketLeaf Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: PocketLeaf keeps everything on your Android phone. We don't have servers, accounts or analytics, the app has no internet permission, and we never collect, sell or share your information.

Who we are

PocketLeaf is an Android app published by Charlie Pradeep (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at charliepradeep@gmail.com.

What PocketLeaf stores, and where

The information you enter is stored only on your phone, inside PocketLeaf's private storage:

  • Bills and subscriptions: names, amounts, dates and notes you type.
  • Your name (optional): if you enter one, it is used only for the greeting on the Home screen.
  • Notes: the text of what you dictate or type. Your voice is not recorded or saved.
  • Vault documents: pages you scan or import, plus titles and expiry dates. Pages are encrypted before they are saved, using a key held in the Android Keystore that does not leave the phone.

None of this is sent to us or to any third party. PocketLeaf needs no account, and the app does not have permission to use the internet.

Permissions the app asks for

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo remind you about bills, notes and expiring documents. Reminders are created and shown on your phone.
Alarms & remindersSo reminders appear at the exact time you chose.
Run at startupTo set your reminders up again after the phone restarts.
MicrophoneTo dictate notes. PocketLeaf hands your speech to the phone's speech recognition service and keeps only the resulting text.
Fingerprint / face / screen lockTo unlock the vault. Biometric data is handled by Android; PocketLeaf never receives it.

Scanning documents uses Google's document scanner, which is part of Google Play services. Importing pictures or PDFs uses Android's file picker, so PocketLeaf sees only the files you select. You can change permissions at any time in Android Settings.

Speech recognition

Dictation is performed by the speech recognition service installed on your phone, not by PocketLeaf. On many phones this runs on the device. On some, the service may send audio to its provider, depending on your phone's settings and language packs. That processing is governed by the provider's terms, not by this policy. PocketLeaf only receives the recognised text.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal data, usage data or diagnostics.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell or share information, because we never receive it.

Backups you create

In Settings you can export your bills and the text of your notes to a file. The file is saved where you choose and is not encrypted. From then on, you control it. Vault documents are not included in exports. PocketLeaf turns off Android's automatic app backup, so nothing is copied to your Google account.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting an item in PocketLeaf removes it, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Because we hold no copy, we can't recover data that you delete or lose.

Children

PocketLeaf does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If a future version of the app collects any data, this policy will say so first.
